# Algorithm research (2026-11-20, after YT Shorts 0 views + TikTok "unoriginal" flag)
Sources are mostly secondary blogs/forums; official items marked OFFICIAL.

## YouTube Shorts
- (OFFICIAL, via secondary coverage) TeamYouTube "Prioritizing original content on Shorts", YouTube Help Community, 2026-10-01
  (https://support.google.com/youtube/blog/470890423/prioritizing-original-content-on-shorts - URL as linked by markstudios, not fetched).
  Less reach for channels "primarily aggregating or re-uploading other creators' videos without significant changes";
  "minor technical edits or template-based changes" don't count; wanted: "your own voice, storytelling, or distinct point of view".
  Rene Ritchie (YouTube): voice-over that just narrates on-screen action does NOT make a clip original. No start date published.
  -> Explains our pattern: first Short 531 views, then every stream re-cut 0-2 views. Recommendations change, not a strike/restriction (none shown in Studio).
- Shorts get a small test audience first; main signals = viewed vs swiped away, % viewed/rewatches, likes/shares (blogs; thresholds disagree).
- New channels: many fresh uploads right away can look bot-like; 0 views on a new 1-sub channel is commonly reported (r/youtube, r/NewTubers anecdotes).
- Hashtags: #shorts NOT required (Shorts = vertical/square, <=3 min). 3-5 relevant tags; only first 3 shown above the title; they categorize, they don't push reach.
- Upload time: no reliable gaming study; blogs contradict each other (weekday afternoon vs 21-24 h). Use our own data.

## TikTok
- "Unoriginal" = imported/reposted without new creative edits; watermark/logo or light trims don't count. Commentary/reaction/
  storytelling are the accepted transformations (blogs summarizing TikTok rules). Short videos also count as low quality.

## Consequences for us
1. Stream re-cuts (crop + captions + title + music, even + slow-mo) are what both platforms now push down. Slow-mo replay helps length, not originality.
2. Real originality levers: our OWN gameplay (owner recording), or formats where the idea is ours (rankings/debates/comparisons with our own
   graphics and text, several sources combined around one take). Single-streamer clips = TikTok only, YT paused or 1/day max.
3. Hashtags: 3-4 relevant (#GTA6 #GTAVI + creator/topic), drop #fyp-style filler on YT.
