## Who I am talking to

One person: an English-speaking GTA 6 player, 16-28, US/UK/EU, who has played the story for a few days and
now searches "GTA 6 easter eggs", "GTA 6 secrets", "GTA 6 mysteries", "GTA 6 hidden locations", "GTA 6
leaks/rumors". Wants to find what their friends missed and loves "wait, that's actually in the game?".
Short attention span, hates filler.

## Language

**English** for every long-form video, title, description and the Shorts/TikToks cut from them.

Faceless channel. Every word is spoken by an ElevenLabs AI voice — **VOICE TBD (owner is choosing a new one,

## Words I never use

Filler only: "in today's video", "without further ado", "let's dive in", "game-changer", "smash that like
button" in the first minute. Nothing else is banned — rumors, leaks, theories and speculation are core topics.

## Rumors, leaks, theories (owner 2026-11-24: allowed, these videos live on them)

- Allowed and encouraged as topics. Always **label** them on screen and in the voice: "RUMOR", "LEAK",
  "THEORY", "unconfirmed" — never presented as confirmed fact.
- Always say **where it comes from** (who posted/reported it) and put the source link in the description.
- Confirmed facts still need a source: official (Rockstar/Take-Two/PlayStation/Xbox, `facts.md`) or visible
  in the footage we show.
- Channel-safety advice (not a ban): don't show stolen/hacked leak files or footage themselves (e.g. the 2022
  hack videos) — Take-Two files copyright strikes on those fast and 3 strikes delete the channel. Talk about
  them, show a credited news screenshot/headline instead.
- Still never: piracy, "play early"/cracks, cheats/exploits that break other players' game, fake giveaways,
  AI-generated "gameplay" passed off as real, implying we are Rockstar.

## Footage (owner 2026-11-24)

- Not recorded by us. Gameplay comes from public clips on X/Twitter, Twitch, YouTube, TikTok — found by the
  owner (sent as links) or by Claude (Twitch API via tools/twitch_top.py + yt-dlp; X links via yt-dlp).
- **Every clip credited**: on screen while it plays (`🎥 @creator`) AND in the description with link.
- Transform, don't re-upload: our voiceover/explanation runs over every segment, keep each source segment short
  (only the part that shows the thing), mix many sources per video. Prefer creators who allow reposts.
- Takedown request -> remove same day (CLAUDE.md hard rule 8).

## My format

- Long-form: 8-12 min "N GTA 6 easter eggs / secrets / mysteries / rumors" lists, one chapter per item. No
  intro, no jingle: open on the most striking frame + the hook. End on the strongest item, then one line to the
  next video.
- Every long video -> 3-5 standalone Shorts/TikToks (`/yt-shorts`), 20-45 s, CLAUDE.md 9:16 + safe-zone rules.
- Description: first 2 lines = what the viewer gets; chapters; "Sources & credits" list; footer
  "Fan page. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games."
- Title/thumbnail: English, label rumors in the title too when the video is mainly rumors ("...RUMORS").
